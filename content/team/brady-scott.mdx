J. Brady Scott, PhD, RRT, is Professor in the Department of Cardiopulmonary Sciences and Program Director for Respiratory Care at Rush. A respiratory therapist for more than two decades with practice experience in adult emergency and critical respiratory care, his research interests include simulation-based education and emergency/critical respiratory care.

He collaborates with Dr. J.C. Rojas and Dr. Kevin Buell on clinical trials and other multidisciplinary projects connected to RICCC.
