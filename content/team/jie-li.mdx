Jie Li, PhD, RRT, is a professor in the Respiratory Care program in Rush University’s College of Health Sciences and a nationally recognized respiratory care researcher. Her scholarship includes aerosol and airway therapies, prone positioning and ARDS, and multi-center clinical research that has informed critical care practice.

She partners with RICCC investigators on clinical trials and other multidisciplinary projects connected to RICCC.
