Jared A. Greenberg, MD, is Associate Professor of Internal Medicine and Assistant Director of the Medical Intensive Care Unit at Rush. His research focuses on sepsis and shock, acute respiratory failure, long-term outcomes after critical illness, shared decision-making and family engagement in the ICU, and medical education.
