Karen E. Jackson, MD, is Assistant Professor of Internal Medicine and Medical Director of the Medical Intensive Care Unit (and Adult ICU Team B) at Rush. Her areas of expertise include airway management and respiratory failure, clinician decision-making, sepsis and septic shock, medical education, COPD, and asthma.

She collaborates with Dr. J.C. Rojas and Dr. Kevin Buell on clinical trials and other multidisciplinary projects connected to RICCC.
