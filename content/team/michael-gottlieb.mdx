Michael Gottlieb, MD, MBA, is Professor of Emergency Medicine, Vice Chair of Research, and Director of the Emergency Ultrasound Division at Rush University Medical Center. He directs research for the American Board of Emergency Medicine and has authored hundreds of peer-reviewed publications spanning medical education, ultrasound, infectious diseases, heart failure, and diversity, equity, and inclusion.

He partners with RICCC investigators on clinical trials and other multidisciplinary projects connected to RICCC.
