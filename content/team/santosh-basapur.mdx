Santosh Basapur, PhD, is Assistant Professor in Family & Preventive Medicine, Director of Design at Rush University, and founder of the Health + Design Integration Lab. Trained in systems design at the IIT Institute of Design, he applies human-centered design to translational science, health care delivery, and patient- and family-centered critical care communication.

He collaborates with Dr. J.C. Rojas and Dr. Kevin Buell on clinical trials and other multidisciplinary design and research projects connected to RICCC.
